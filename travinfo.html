Travel Information

Worried about visas? Don't worry, check this website to see if you need a visa,
and you also find the visa fee and processing period.

Prior to entering or leaving Brunei, visitors must fill out an E-arrival card, linked here.

Be sure to keep the customs and regulations in mind when visiting.
Including but not limited to alcohol limitations, goods declarations,
as well as etiquette when visiting historical or cultural sites

The weather in Brunei is best described as a constant summer,
with heavier rain during the Northeast Monsoon seasons.
Be sure to pack the appropriate clothes and items accordingly.
